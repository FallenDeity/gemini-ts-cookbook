Our Departments

  • Sales

    Our Sales department is responsible for generating revenue by selling our paper products to customers. They work closely with clients to understand their needs and offer the best solutions.

  • Marketing

    The Marketing department promotes our brand and products through various channels, including advertising, public relations, and social media. They develop and execute marketing campaigns to reach our target audience.

  • Production

    Our Production department oversees the manufacturing process of our paper products, ensuring quality and efficiency. They manage our production facilities and work to optimize our operations.

  • Finance

    The Finance department manages the company's financial resources, including accounting, budgeting, and financial reporting. They ensure the financial health of the company and provide timely and accurate financial information.

  • Research & Development

    Our Research & Development department focuses on innovation and developing new paper products and technologies. They conduct research, develop prototypes, and test new products to meet the evolving needs of the market.
